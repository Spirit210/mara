Step 1 — Detect

Label it, out loud or in your head:

"I am experiencing an urge."

Naming it engages the prefrontal cortex. That's the whole job of this step.

Step 2 — Predict

The mechanism step — expectancy violation is what makes the learning happen.

State your prediction explicitly. Without a prediction, there is nothing to violate.

Step 3 — Rate

110

External or internal?

Step 4 — Sit

00:00

Re-rate every ~5 minutes. Watch the number change.

Body scan: where do you feel it? Chest? Stomach? Jaw? Just notice. Neutral language: "I notice a pulling sensation…" Your only job is to observe.
No safety behaviors during Sit. No cold shower, no distraction, no "just edging." Your brain must credit your capacity — not a crutch — or no learning happens.
